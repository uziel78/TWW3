# Dark Elf (Druchii) mods

Built and tested with Mixu's Unlocker. Copy a `.pack` into the game's `data` folder and enable it.

| Pack | What it does | New campaign? |
|---|---|---|
| `Druchii_Units_MOD.pack` | Extra abilities and attributes for Dark Elf units (Black Guard, Bleakswords, Dark Riders, Shades, Khainite Assassin) | No |
| `Druchii_Naggarond_MOD.pack` | Malekith's Naggarond: new starting army, slave tribute from other Dark Elves, new faction effects, the Decrees of the Witch King | Yes |
| `Druchii_Malekith_MOD.pack` | Malekith's lord effects: Slaves to the Throne, Formation Attack, Malekith's Legion; stronger Circlet of Iron | Recommended |
| `!!Druchii_Malekith_MOD_faction_screen.pack` | Hides an empty lord-effect line on the faction screen. **Needs Mixu's Unlocker** | - |
| `Druchii_Cult-of-Pleasure_MOD.pack` | Morathi's Cult of Pleasure: Slaanesh-themed roster, buildings, Mark of Slaanesh, the Sacrifice to Slaanesh rite, pacts with N'Kari and the Cult of Excess | Yes |
| `Druchii_Morathi_MOD.pack` | Morathi's lord effects: cheaper Heroes in her army, growth in the province she is in | No |
